## Interview Flow

### 1. SEO Strategy Interview

**Opening:** "Based on your positioning in [category] and your [Persona 1]'s pain points, let me help build your keyword strategy."

**Pre-suggest 3-5 keyword clusters** derived from positioning + ICP pain points. For each cluster, suggest 4-5 keywords with estimated search intent (informational, navigational, commercial, transactional) and funnel stage (top, middle, bottom).

**Questions (ask one at a time):**

1. "Here are keyword clusters I derived from your positioning and ICP pain points: [show clusters]. Do these resonate, or should I adjust? Any keywords you specifically want to own?"
2. "Do you have existing content (blog, docs, guides) we should map keywords to?"
3. "What's your current domain authority / SEO starting position? (If you don't know, that's fine — I'll assume early stage.)"
4. "What publications or sites should you be featured in for backlinks? Any existing relationships?"
5. "What are your competitors ranking for that you aren't? (I can suggest based on your competitor analysis if you have one.)"
6. "What are your traffic and ranking targets? (e.g., 10K organic visits/month in 12 months, page 1 for [keyword])"

**Populate:**
- Keyword clusters with individual keywords, search volume estimates, intent, funnel stage
- Content mapping (keyword to page/article)
- Link building strategy with target publications and outreach approach
- Competitor keyword gap analysis
- Technical SEO checklist
- Metrics targets with timeline

After writing: "SEO strategy saved with [N] keyword clusters and [N] target keywords. Moving to paid campaigns."

### 2. Paid Campaigns Interview

**Questions (ask one at a time):**

1. "What channels have you tested or want to test? (LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads, Meta, Reddit, Twitter/X, etc.)"
2. "What's your monthly ad budget? (I'll suggest allocation by channel based on your ICP.)"
3. "What's your target CAC? Target ROAS? (I'd suggest [X] based on your pricing and LTV.)"
4. "What type of ads have worked best?" — suggest based on ICP: "Your [Persona 1] spends time on LinkedIn, so LinkedIn Sponsored Content targeting [title] at [company size] companies is likely your best bet."
5. "Do you have a retargeting strategy? (e.g., website visitors, email list, lookalikes)"

**Populate:**
- Channel selection with rationale tied to ICP behavior
- Audience targeting using ICP firmographic and demographic data
- Ad copy templates written in brand voice (2-3 per channel)
- Budget allocation by channel with expected CPL/CPA
- Conversion tracking setup (pixels, UTMs, attribution model)
- Retargeting flows (website visitors, engaged leads, abandoned demo signups)
- A/B testing roadmap

After writing: "Paid campaigns strategy saved for [channels] with $[budget] monthly allocation. Moving to campaign calendar."

### 3. Campaign Calendar Interview

**Opening:** "Let me set up a monthly campaign rhythm based on your channels and content pillars."

**Pre-fill a quarterly calendar template** with suggested campaign types based on their channels, content pillars, and any events mentioned. Include:
- Content campaigns tied to each pillar
- Paid campaign flights
- Email nurture sequences
- Launch/announcement windows

**Questions:**

1. "Here's a quarterly campaign rhythm I've drafted. Does this cadence feel right — monthly, quarterly, or tied to product launches?"
2. "Any recurring events I should build in? (conferences, product releases, seasonal patterns in your industry)"
3. "What does a launch checklist look like for you? (I'll create a reusable template.)"

**Populate:**
- Quarterly campaign calendar with specific dates/weeks
- Campaign types mapped to content pillars
- Launch checklist template
- Resource requirements per campaign type
- Cross-channel coordination notes

After writing: "Campaign calendar saved with quarterly rhythm and launch checklist. Moving to engagement strategy."

### 4. Engagement Strategy Interview

**Opening:** "Your ICP hangs out at [places from ICP]. Let's build an engagement strategy."

**Questions (ask one at a time):**

1. "How much time per day can you (or your team) spend on LinkedIn engagement? (15 min, 30 min, 1 hour)"
2. "What types of accounts do you want to be visible to? (Name specific target account types — e.g., 'Series A SaaS CTOs', 'Enterprise procurement leaders')"
3. "Do you have a DM strategy or just public engagement? (I'd suggest a warm DM approach after 3+ public interactions.)"
4. "How do you measure LinkedIn engagement success? (profile views, connection acceptance rate, inbound DMs, content impressions)"

**Populate:**
- Target account categories with example accounts to engage
- Daily engagement routine with time blocks
- Comment strategy (types of comments, tone, value-add approach)
- DM sequences (connection request, follow-up, value offer)
- Community building tactics (groups, events, newsletter)
- Tracking metrics with targets

After writing: "Engagement strategy saved targeting [ICP persona] accounts. Moving to post templates."

### 5. Post Templates Interview

**Opening:** "Based on your [N] content pillars and brand voice, I'll generate post templates you can reuse."

**Generate 5-6 post templates**, each using the user's actual voice parameters and tone from brand-voice.md:

1. **Story-driven template** — for personal/behind-the-scenes pillar. Hook with a moment, build tension, deliver insight.
2. **Data-driven template** — for insight pillar. Lead with a surprising stat, provide context, draw conclusion.
3. **Contrarian/hot-take template** — for bold positioning. Challenge conventional wisdom, back it up, reframe.
4. **How-to/tactical template** — for educational pillar. Promise a specific outcome, deliver numbered steps.
5. **Social proof/case study template** — for credibility. Setup the problem, show the transformation, reveal the method.
6. **Engagement/question template** — for community building. Pose a genuine question, share your perspective, invite responses.

**Show all templates and ask:**
"Here are 6 post templates in your voice. Which formats do you want to keep, modify, or replace? Any template types I'm missing that you use regularly?"

**Populate:**
- Each template with structure, example hooks, and fill-in sections
- Content pillar mapping (which template works best for which pillar)
- Posting cadence recommendation
- Best practices for each format
- Hook formulas

After writing: "Post templates saved with [N] reusable formats in your brand voice."

## Important Guidelines

- Ask questions ONE AT A TIME. Do not dump all questions at once.
- Pre-generate as much as possible from existing context, then ask the user to validate/adjust.
- Use specific data from their positioning, ICP, content pillars, and brand voice — never use generic placeholders.
- Write ad copy and post templates in the user's actual brand voice, not generic marketing speak.
- If the user says "skip" for any file, mark it as skipped and move to the next.
- If the user seems unsure, offer smart defaults and let them accept or modify.
- Write each file immediately after completing its interview section — do not wait until the end.
