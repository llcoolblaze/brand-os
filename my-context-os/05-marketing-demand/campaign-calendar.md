## Campaign Launch Checklist

Use this checklist for every campaign launch. Copy to a new section per campaign.

### Pre-Launch (2+ weeks before)
- [ ] Campaign brief completed (goal, audience, channels, timeline, budget)
- [ ] Assets created (copy, design, landing page, emails)
- [ ] Landing page live and tested (form works, tracking fires, mobile-friendly)
- [ ] UTM parameters defined and documented
- [ ] Email sequences built and reviewed
- [ ] Ad campaigns set up in platform (targeting, budget, creative)
- [ ] CRM/automation workflows configured (lead routing, scoring, notifications)
- [ ] Internal team briefed (sales knows what's coming and how to follow up)

### Launch Day
- [ ] All ads go live
- [ ] First email sent
- [ ] LinkedIn post published
- [ ] Landing page final check (load time, form, tracking)
- [ ] Confirm leads are flowing into CRM correctly
- [ ] Sales team notified: "Campaign is live"

### Post-Launch (ongoing)
- [ ] Daily: Monitor ad spend and performance
- [ ] Daily: Check lead quality (are the right people converting?)
- [ ] Weekly: Optimize underperforming ads/emails
- [ ] Weekly: Sales feedback on lead quality
- [ ] End of campaign: Run post-campaign analysis (see below)
