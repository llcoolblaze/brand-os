# Fundraising Preparation

> Fundraising is a sales process. You're selling equity in your vision. This document
> organizes everything you need: the narrative, the metrics, the materials, and the
> preparation for investor conversations.

## Data Room Checklist

Prepare these before starting the raise. Investors will request them during diligence.

### Corporate Documents
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation / Articles
- [ ] Bylaws / Operating Agreement
- [ ] Cap table (current, fully diluted)
- [ ] Previous funding documents (SAFEs, convertible notes, prior rounds)
- [ ] Board meeting minutes (if applicable)
- [ ] IP assignments for all founders and employees

### Financial Documents
- [ ] Historical financials (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow)
- [ ] Financial projections (3-year model, bottoms-up)
- [ ] Monthly MRR / revenue breakdown
- [ ] Burn rate and runway calculation
- [ ] Bank statements (last 6 months)

### Business Documents
- [ ] Pitch deck (current version)
- [ ] One-pager / executive summary
- [ ] Customer list with logos and contract values
- [ ] Key customer contracts / LOIs
- [ ] Partnership agreements
- [ ] Product roadmap

### Team Documents
- [ ] Org chart
- [ ] Founder bios / LinkedIn profiles
- [ ] Key employee agreements
- [ ] Advisor agreements
- [ ] Employee option pool details

### Legal / Compliance
- [ ] Any pending or potential litigation
- [ ] Material contracts
- [ ] Insurance policies
- [ ] Regulatory compliance documentation (if applicable)

## Narrative Arc

Your fundraising story should follow this arc:

1. **Hook:** A compelling insight or contrarian observation about the market
2. **Problem:** The specific pain that creates the opportunity
3. **Insight:** What you understand that others don't (your "secret")
4. **Solution:** How you've built something that leverages that insight
5. **Proof:** Traction that validates the insight + solution
6. **Vision:** Where this goes -- the 10x bigger version
7. **Ask:** What you need to get there
