# Market Positioning

> Your positioning is the strategic context you set before anyone hears your pitch.
> It answers: What category are you in? What problem do you solve? Why are you the
> best choice? This document should be the single source of truth that informs all
> messaging across content, sales, and marketing.

## Unique Value Proposition

<!-- TODO: Fill in the framework below -->

**For** [target customer -- from ICP]
**who** [statement of need or opportunity]
**our product is a** [product category]
**that** [key benefit / reason to buy].
**Unlike** [competitive alternative],
**we** [primary differentiation].

### Filled Example (delete or replace):

> **For** mid-market B2B SaaS companies with 10-50 sales reps
> **who** need accurate revenue forecasting without relying on rep self-reporting,
> **our product is a** revenue intelligence platform
> **that** automatically captures and analyzes buyer engagement signals to predict deal outcomes.
> **Unlike** Clari or Gong, which require extensive setup and only work with large data sets,
> **we** deliver actionable forecasts within 14 days of implementation with as few as 10 active deals.
