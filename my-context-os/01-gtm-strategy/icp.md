# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

> This is the most cross-referenced document in your Brand OS. Your ICP defines WHO
> you are building for, selling to, and creating content for. Every outbound sequence,
> content pillar, pricing decision, and sales play flows from this document. Treat it
> as the foundation -- if this is wrong, everything downstream is wrong.

## ICP Validation Checklist

Review this quarterly to ensure your ICP is still accurate.

- [ ] Analyzed last quarter's closed-won deals -- do they match the ICP?
- [ ] Analyzed last quarter's churned customers -- were they outside ICP?
- [ ] Reviewed lost deals -- were they ICP companies we failed to close, or bad fit?
- [ ] Updated firmographic criteria based on new data
- [ ] Validated persona pain points against recent discovery calls
