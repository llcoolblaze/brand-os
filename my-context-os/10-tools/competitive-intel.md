## Competitive Monitoring

### Sources to Monitor

| Source | What to Look For | Frequency |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| Competitor websites | Pricing changes, new features, messaging shifts | Monthly |

| Crunchbase / PitchBook | Funding rounds, acquisitions | Monthly |
