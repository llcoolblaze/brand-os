## Comment Strategy

### The 3 Types of Comments That Work

| Type | When to Use | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Add value** | When you have a genuine insight to contribute | "This resonates. One thing I'd add from our experience: [specific insight that extends their point]." |
| **Share experience** | When their post relates to something you've lived | "We hit this exact wall at [company]. What worked for us was [specific tactic]. The counterintuitive part was..." |
| **Ask a smart question** | When you want to start a dialogue | "Curious about your take on [related angle]. We've been debating [specific question] internally." |

### Comment Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Comment within the first 60 minutes of their post (algorithm boost)
- Write 3+ sentences (substantial comments get more visibility)
- Reference their specific point, not generic praise
- Share a genuine perspective or experience
- End with a question to continue the conversation

**Don't:**
- "Great post!" (adds zero value, looks like a bot)
- Pitch your product in comments (instant credibility kill)
- Copy-paste the same comment on multiple posts
- Only comment on prospect posts (be a real community member)
- Argue or be combative (even if you disagree, be respectful)

## DM Sequences

### Warm DM Framework

Only DM people you've been engaging with for 1-2 weeks minimum. Cold DMs from
strangers get ignored.

**Step 1: Connection Request (after 3+ meaningful comment interactions)**
```
Hey [Name] -- been enjoying your posts on [topic], especially [specific reference].
Would love to connect.
```

**Step 2: Thank You + Value (after they accept, wait 1-2 days)**
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Saw your post about [topic] -- really resonated
because we've been [relevant experience]. Thought you might find [this resource]
useful.
```

**Step 3: Soft Conversation Starter (3-5 days later)**
```
Quick question -- [relevant question about their work/challenge that shows genuine
curiosity, not a pitch setup]. Curious how you're thinking about it.
```

**Step 4: Bridge to Conversation (only if they engage)**
```
This is a topic I'm deep in -- we've been [relevant work]. Would you be open to
a quick chat? Not a pitch -- genuinely interested in comparing notes.
```

### DM Rules
- Never pitch in DMs without an invitation
- Personalize every single message
- If they don't respond, don't follow up more than once
- Keep messages under 3 sentences
- Provide value before asking for anything
