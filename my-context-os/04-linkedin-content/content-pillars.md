# Content Pillars

> Content pillars are the 3-5 thematic categories that define what you talk about.
> Every piece of content should map to a pillar. Pillars ensure consistency, prevent
> random acts of content, and build authority in specific areas over time.

---

## Pillar Selection Criteria

Each pillar should meet ALL of these:
- [ ] Aligns with your positioning (see [positioning.md](../01-gtm-strategy/positioning.md))
- [ ] Your ICP cares about this topic (see [icp.md](../01-gtm-strategy/icp.md))
- [ ] You have genuine expertise or a unique perspective
- [ ] You can create 10+ pieces of content in this pillar without repeating
- [ ] It supports your business goal (demand gen, authority, hiring, etc.)
