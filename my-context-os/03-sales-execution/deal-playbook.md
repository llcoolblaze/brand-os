# Deal Playbook

> This playbook defines how to move a qualified opportunity from discovery to close.
> It codifies the repeatable motions, frameworks, and tactics that your best reps
> use so that every rep can execute at a high level.

---

## Deal Stages & Actions

Each stage maps to the pipeline stages in [pipeline-stages.md](../02-outbound-systems/pipeline-stages.md).

### Stage 1: Discovery

**Objective:** Validate the opportunity is real and worth pursuing.

**Actions:**
1. Run discovery using [discovery-questions.md](./discovery-questions.md)
2. Identify the primary pain and quantify impact
3. Map the buying process (who, what, when, how)
4. Determine if there's a compelling event / timeline
5. Identify potential champion

**Deliverable:** Completed qualification scorecard (see framework below)

**Exit criteria:** Pain confirmed, authority mapped, timeline <6 months

### Stage 2: Evaluation

**Objective:** Demonstrate that your solution solves their specific problem.

**Actions:**
1. Deliver a tailored demo (not a feature tour -- a solution presentation)
2. Build a mutual action plan with the champion
3. Identify all stakeholders and their concerns
4. Provide relevant case studies (match industry/size/pain)
5. Offer a pilot/POC if appropriate (define success criteria upfront)

**Deliverable:** Demo deck customized to their pain points, mutual action plan

**Exit criteria:** Champion confirms "this solves our problem" + decision maker engaged

### Stage 3: Proposal

**Objective:** Present a compelling business case and negotiate terms.

**Actions:**
1. Send proposal with ROI calculation
2. Present pricing (see [pricing.md](../01-gtm-strategy/pricing.md))
3. Handle objections (see [objection-handling.md](./objection-handling.md))
4. Navigate procurement / legal
5. Multi-thread if single-threaded

**Deliverable:** Proposal document, ROI model

**Exit criteria:** Verbal commitment from decision maker

### Stage 4: Close

**Objective:** Get the contract signed and transition to onboarding.

**Actions:**
1. Send contract / order form
2. Align on legal redlines (have pre-approved alternatives ready)
3. Confirm payment terms and billing setup
4. Schedule onboarding kickoff
5. Introduce customer success contact

**Deliverable:** Signed contract

## Champion Building

### What Makes a Champion

A real champion has ALL of these:
- [ ] **Power:** Can influence the decision maker
- [ ] **Pain:** Personally affected by the problem you solve
- [ ] **Vision:** Sees how your solution transforms their work
- [ ] **Access:** Willing to give you information and introductions
- [ ] **Risk tolerance:** Willing to advocate internally (their reputation is on the line)

### How to Build a Champion

1. **Give them ammunition:** Create internal-facing materials they can share
   - One-pager customized to their company
   - ROI calculator pre-filled with their numbers
   - Competitive comparison if they're evaluating alternatives
2. **Coach them:** Prep them for internal conversations
   - "When you present this to [CFO], they'll likely ask about X. Here's how to answer..."
3. **Protect them:** Never make them look bad
4. **Celebrate them:** Give them credit for the initiative

### Champion Test
> Ask: "Can you walk me through what happens between our next call and a signed contract?"
> If they can't articulate the internal process, they may not be a real champion.

## Multi-Threading Strategy

Never be single-threaded on a deal. If your champion leaves, the deal dies.

**Multi-threading tactics:**
1. Ask champion for introductions: "Would it help to include [role] in our next call?"
2. Send relevant content directly to other stakeholders (with champion's blessing)
3. Engage other personas at the company through LinkedIn
4. Host a "lunch and learn" or workshop that naturally includes multiple stakeholders

## Proposal Template

<!-- TODO: Customize your proposal structure -->

**Standard Proposal Outline:**
1. **Executive Summary** (1 page) -- Their problem, our solution, expected outcome
2. **Current State** -- What we learned in discovery (use their words)
3. **Recommended Solution** -- How we solve it (not features -- outcomes)
4. **Implementation Plan** -- Timeline, milestones, responsibilities
5. **Investment** -- Pricing, payment terms, what's included
6. **ROI Projection** -- Quantified business impact
7. **Social Proof** -- 2-3 relevant case studies
8. **Next Steps** -- Clear path to signed contract with dates

### Close Acceleration Tactics
- **Create urgency:** Tie to their compelling event, not arbitrary discounts
- **Remove friction:** Pre-negotiate common legal redlines
- **Reduce risk:** Offer pilot, money-back guarantee, or phased rollout
- **Social proof at close:** Introduce them to a reference customer
